The product, now named Merge, is a premium dating application focused on creating intelligent connections through data-driven compatibility metrics. It has evolved from its initial mystical/astrology theme (Cosme) into a sophisticated, tech-forward platform.

**Core User Experience:**
*   **Branding:** A clean, minimalist aesthetic with a pure black background, white text, and Intelligent Blue & Confident Purple accents. The logo is a custom merge wordmark with a unique 'rg' ligature.

*   **Merge Score:** The central matching metric, replacing Compatibility Score. It provides an overall percentage and has an AMI-based glow system (Cyan, Purple, Blue) to indicate match strength. Tapping the score reveals a detailed breakdown modal explaining the compatibility across different categories (e.g., Lifestyle, Emotional Rhythm).
*   **Trust Score:** A Connection Integrity score is displayed as a tiered title (e.g., Reliable, Building Trust), with the exact percentage available on hover.
*   **Cosmic Insights & AI Starters:** An expandable section on the profile card provides AI-generated conversation starters and Cosmic Insights to explain the why behind a match.
*   **Aura Lab:** The in-app store, rebranded from Cosmic Store, allows users to purchase items.
*   **Login:** A modern, premium login page designed to reflect the app's intelligent and secure positioning.
